# FitShot app

Scope: the whole working app. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience and job: a non-technical small brand owner (often Gen Z, phone-first) produces catalog photos and decides what is safe to publish.
Proof: real pipeline results only. No invented customers, benchmarks or savings.
Constraints: status never relies on color alone, photos sit on neutral grounds, provisional thresholds stay disclosed.
Pinned by the user (2026-11-24): "Editorial & Visual Discovery", premium but funky, replacing the sketchbook round. Earlier answers still hold: grids, tables and fields stay crisp; no neon overload; photos never look tinted.
Re-pinned by the user (2026-11-25): a 60/30/10 color split, keeping cobalt as the 30% and adding neon volt lime #C8FF2E as the 10%, "to look less like AI slop". Volt is only ever a filled shape with ink on it.
Chosen direction: Lookbook. Memorable moment: a photo opens "The Edit", a white inspection card with a cobalt masthead and a cover-burst status sticker, beside an image-first discovery grid.

## Direction contract

THESIS: FitShot as a fashion lookbook's photo desk: big images first, magazine-cover type, and every photo carrying its edit. Refuses the grey SaaS dashboard with QA as a small badge.

OWN-WORLD: White paper #ffffff and cool grey #f1f1ef, ink #0e0e10, one committed color: cobalt #2b35f5 owning the navigation rail, the Edit masthead and selection. Anybody variable at extreme widths for mastheads and titles (expanded black), Bricolage Grotesque for the work. Sharp 2px corners, hairline rules, borderless photos on neutral mats. Status as cover-burst stickers carrying the word. Dark theme: ink paper #0d0d10; the cobalt rail and white Edit card stay the same objects.

STORY: The owner scans a big image grid, opens a photo's Edit, reads the problem in plain words, then approves, retries or rejects.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cobalt rail at left (expanded FITSHOT masthead, nav, review count, theme). Expanded job title as a cover line with counts and Export, over a discovery grid of large borderless photos, the first one featured larger. The Edit docked at right: cobalt masthead, burst status, eight ruled checks, decision buttons. On phones the rail becomes a cobalt bottom bar and the Edit opens as a dialog.

FORM: User-pinned editorial world over the incumbent workspace topology (photo grid plus docked inspection), position 1. Signature move: the Edit card with its cover-burst status. seed:6c49d8ac992bac57 (rolled after the user pinned Lookbook, for provenance only; the roll did not choose this direction)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
